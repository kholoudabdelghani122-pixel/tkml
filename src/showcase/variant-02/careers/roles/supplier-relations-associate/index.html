Role preview

Supplier Relations Associate

Tripoli, Libya · Full-time · Commercial operations Preview role · Not approved for publication

Support thoughtful supplier engagement and make commercial communication easier to follow.

Role details

  • Collect and organise supplier profiles, credentials, and product-source information.
  • Coordinate introductions, questions, and follow-ups between buyers, suppliers, and TKML colleagues.
  • Track agreed actions and help clarify responsibilities and outstanding documentation.
  • Treat business and contact information with care and confidentiality.

Requirements

  • Experience in supplier relations, customer service, business operations, or coordination.
  • A courteous, reliable communication style and strong follow-through.
  • Good organisation skills and comfort maintaining records accurately.
  • Professional Arabic and English communication; additional languages are welcome.
