Bringing the right businesses together.

Finding a suitable supplier or commercial partner takes time. We help businesses define what they need, review their options, and coordinate the discussions that move an opportunity forward.

Based in Tripoli, Libya, tkml Systems is a technology-enabled B2B trade intermediary. Our team brings more than 70 years of combined professional experience.

How we help

What we do

We help buyers turn purchase requirements into a sourcing plan, identify and screen potential suppliers, and compare quotations. Once the parties are ready to talk, we help clarify terms, coordinate documentation, and follow up on agreed actions.

We also support businesses exploring new markets, franchise opportunities, and commercial partnerships. We start by understanding what each business needs from a partner, then make relevant introductions and support the initial discussions.

Our operating principle

Standards Before Transactions

Trust starts with knowing who you’re dealing with, what you’re agreeing to, and who’s responsible for each step. We review available supplier credentials and product-source information, clarify commercial terms, and document responsibilities. We disclose applicable commissions and potential conflicts of interest, and we handle confidential information with care.

We step away from opportunities that would require us to compromise these standards.
